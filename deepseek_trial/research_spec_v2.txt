GOLD-ORB-v2
Status: unvalidated research specification.
This file freezes the rules before anyone measures them.
It is not a claim that the rules make money, and it is not permission to raise risk.

Job of the system
  Fixed code decides whether the data is usable, whether a setup exists,
  where the stop and target go, and how large the trade may be.
  A language model may explain the decision afterward.
  It may not change the direction, the stop, the size, or a NO-TRADE.

Market
  The tradable price is the exact XAUUSD symbol on the FundedNext MT5 account.
  Yahoo GC=F, a saved snapshot, and a daily yield are background only.
  If the MT5 bars or the current bid/ask are missing or old: DATA_INVALID, then NO-TRADE.
  The last closed 5-minute bar must be no older than 6 minutes.
  A live quote must be no older than 5 seconds.
  The candle that is still forming is never a signal.

Bars
  Closed 5-minute bars for the setup.
  Closed 1-hour bars only when the hourly filter is turned on.
  A higher-timeframe candle that has not closed yet is ignored.

Session, Europe/London, weekdays
  Opening range: 08:00 up to but not including 08:30.
  The range needs all six 5-minute bars. A missing bar cancels the day.
  Entries: 08:30 up to but not including 10:30.
  Flat by 11:00. No new trade at the deadline.

Setup
  Breakout: a closed 5-minute bar closes outside the opening range.
  Retest: within the next three 5-minute bars, price touches the broken edge
  and the bar closes back on the breakout side.
  The breakout bar itself is not the retest.
  Entry in a replay: the open of the next 5-minute bar, treated as a mid,
  then worsened by half the spread and by slippage.
  Entry live: the MT5 ask for a long, or the bid for a short, plus slippage.
  One filled trade per day. No adding, no averaging.
  Once a setup is filled, rejected, or expired, a later cycle cannot rebuild it.

Hourly filter, variant orb_h1_trend (the default specification)
  Long: last closed 1-hour close is above EMA50, and EMA20 is above EMA50.
  Short: the mirror image.
  If those hourly values are not ready, NO-TRADE.
  Variant orb_only is the same setup with this filter turned off.
  Test orb_only and orb_h1_trend separately. Do not blend them into a third rule.
  VWAP is not part of this version.

Stop, target, costs
  Stop: just beyond the retest bar's extreme by 0.10 times the closed 5-minute ATR14.
  A trade with no protective stop is invalid.
  Target: 2R from the actual entry to that stop.
  No entry if that target sits beyond the prior day's opposing high (longs)
  or opposing low (shorts). The prior day must already be known.
  No entry if estimated round-trip friction is more than 0.10R.
  If the stop and the target could both have been hit on the same bar, count the stop.
  If the bar opens through the stop, exit at the open, not at the stop.

Risk on a $25,000 account
  Starting risk budget: 0.25% of the starting balance, which is $62.50.
  Do not size from the 1% ($250) cap.
  The budget is then the smallest of:
    that $62.50,
    daily room left under the $750 hard loss, minus a $25 buffer,
    room left above the $23,500 equity floor, minus a $25 buffer,
    room left in the one-idea cap, minus a $10 buffer.
  Open trades and pending orders count as exposure. A second idea is NO-TRADE.
  Round the lot size down. If the smallest lot does not fit, NO-TRADE.
  Live sizing should use MT5's profit and margin calculations when the terminal
  is connected. This code never calls order_send.

News
  The calendar file is mandatory. If it is missing or unreadable: NO-TRADE.
  No new entry from 30 minutes before a timed event until 30 minutes after.
  An event marked tag_only does not block.
  An event marked block_afternoon stays blocked until 21:00 New York.
  A position is closed from 15 minutes before the event.
  Profit earned while the hold overlaps a restricted window is not counted as
  payout-eligible profit. A loss still counts.
  Headlines are deduplicated notes. A headline cannot approve a trade.
  A headline with no timestamp is unusable as a timed event.

Account
  MT5 balance, equity, margin, positions, pending orders, and deal history
  are the authority.
  The handwritten trade backlog is a note. It does not set equity or loss room.
  A withdrawal is not a trading loss. If the cash movements do not reconcile
  to the balance within $1, NO-TRADE.
  If the broker's daily reset time is unknown, NO-TRADE.
  If any open or pending order has no stop, NO-TRADE.
  Holds shorter than 5 minutes are flagged. Gains on those holds are not
  counted as payout-eligible until the account terms confirm the rule.
  Automation stays manual in this package even if a flag says an EA might be allowed.

What is recorded
  A signal row (created, rejected, or expired) is not a trade result.
  An outcome row is written once: fill, stop, target, exit reason, cost,
  slippage, the worst and best move while open, rule flags, and payout-eligible
  profit. Comparing this signal with the previous signal is not an evaluation.

What this version refuses to do
  It does not treat a 50/20/12/10/8 score as an edge.
  It does not treat yesterday's real yield as a five-minute confirmation.
  It does not let a saved snapshot approve a fresh trade.
  It does not let an explanation override a failed check.
  It does not add VWAP, and it does not mix several strategies into one system
  before any one of them has been measured on its own.
