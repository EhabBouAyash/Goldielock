DXY above 100: Strong dollar, weak gold
DXY below 100: Weak dollar, strong gold. 

Real yields:
     in positive range: Inflation isnt catching up to cash based
investments, like stocks and bonds, as fast as they are growing. 
So this means the dollar holds its value through inflation. This indicates
that gold might be weaker. 

    in negative range: it means that inflation is catching up too fast to
the purchasing power of the dollar --> cash investments are weaker.
Gold becomes a stronger investment.
